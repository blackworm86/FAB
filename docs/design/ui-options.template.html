FAB: The Bulge, interface options

Three ways to drop the sidebar and play from the board itself. Try each one on the same three moments: moving a block, setting up a battle by dragging assets onto it, and the dice roll. Everything on the stage is clickable or draggable.

The same flow in every option

1 PrepareReinforcements arrive and chits are drawn on their own. You only get a prompt if there is a choice: place a Task Force, play an event, use an engineer.
2 MovePick a block, see every legal destination, hover one for the exact route, MP cost and fuel. Click to commit. Strategic and reserve moves are the same gesture.
3 FightPick a contested area. The camera jumps in. Drag assets onto the sides, read the odds, roll.
4 BreakthroughAppears only when someone has reserves, exploitation markers or Special Actions to use. Otherwise it is skipped.
5 SupplyAutomatic. A summary slides in: control changes, unsupplied blocks, morale checks. One click to continue.
