# Rules audit, 8 November 2026

A full check of the digital game against the rulebooks: the Series Rules and the exclusive, optional and scenario rules of The Bulge, Sicily and Golan '73, the game loop, and the table's buttons. This file says what was checked, what was wrong, what was fixed, what was left alone and why, and what needs the owner's ruling.

## 1. How the audit was done

- The rule text came from the game's own rules reader (`data/<game>/rules.json`, built from the PDFs and the proofread Play Books).
- Three reviewers read every rule section of one volume each and compared it with the engine code, section by section (about 480 sections). Each suspected problem had to be reproduced with a small test against the engine before it counted. The reviewers' raw reports are not in the repository; their confirmed findings are all listed below.
- A fourth reviewer drove the table in a real browser (Playwright) for all three volumes: whole turns through the red button, battles to the end, card plays, undo, the sandbox. Its automated clicks on the 3D board were unreliable when the camera had to pan, so every "button did nothing" report was re-checked by hand before being believed.
- Every fix below has a test in `engine/test/bulge-audit.test.ts`, `sicily-audit.test.ts` or `golan-audit.test.ts` (35 tests), and the whole suite passes: engine 394 tests (including the random full-game fuzzer), web 122.

## 2. What was fixed

Severity: **High** = a wrong result or an illegal play in ordinary games; **Medium** = an edge case or wrong timing; **Low** = text or a rare corner.

### Series Rules (all volumes)

| Rule | Severity | What was wrong | What the game does now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.34 | High | A unit leaving a contested area could move on into an area held only by an enemy garrison or defended roadblock, creating a second contested area. | Areas held by enemy assets alone count as enemy-held for the "no new contested area" rule. |
| 5.35c | High | A bridge used by a reaction move stayed "used" into the Breakthrough Movement Phase, blocking the other side. | The one-unit-per-bridge count restarts with each movement phase. |
| 5.35d | High | Once a unit had entered a newly contested area overland, a second unit crossing a river into it could not be shot at (no bridge-blowing attempt). | The first bridge crossing into a newly contested area always triggers the attempt. |
| 8.1 / 8.22 | High | A single-use Special Action of any army paid for a Breakthrough combat round. | The event must belong to the attackers' Higher Echelon. |
| 6.2 / 6.26 | Medium | When the last attacker in good order died and only a disordered attacker remained, the battle went on with a dead point unit. | The battle ends ("no attacker left in good order"). |
| 6.6 (2016) | Medium | A unit overstacked at the end of a phase was eliminated when all neighbours were full. | It retreats past the full areas to the nearest area with room, as the 2016 rule says. |
| 4.35 / 8.23 | Medium | A battle whose only defender was a garrison or Task Force could be reinforced by Special Action. | Refused, like a defended roadblock. The reinforcing unit must also be able to cross into the area (restricted boundary, British limits). |
| 5.23 | Low | Field works survived with only a garrison in the area. | They need a unit (block). |
| 19.4 | Low | On the last turn a big lead was reported as "Sudden death" instead of the scenario's result table. | No sudden-death check on the last turn. |
| Game loop | Low | When "End phase" was refused (battle open, areas to fight), both players' "done" marks were lost. | The marks survive a refused end of phase. |

### The Bulge

| Rule | Severity | What was wrong | What the game does now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16.21 / 16.22 | High | With one ground hit the defender could retreat for free: the hit paid for the retreat and the garrison died "as a hit" on top, so one hit took Bastogne. | The garrison (or Task Force) is destroyed first; the units may retreat only if a hit is left over. With a single hit there is no retreat option. |
| 16.11 | High | Von der Heydte rolled out-of-supply checks from the turn it dropped (it is immune until turn 4), and twice a turn from turn 4. | Only the 16.11 check, from turn 4. |
| 16.22 | High | An unplaced Task Force in the Available Box could be assigned to any battle as a mobile asset, and a Task Force kept back could never be placed later. | Task Forces are placed in the Reinforcement Phase only; one kept back can be placed on a later turn. |
| 17.1 / 15.1c | High | German army Special Action events did not work for Strategic Reserve units in that army's zone, and did work for the army's own units outside the zone. | Events work in their army's zone, for the army's units and the Strategic Reserve units standing there. |
| 17.11 | High | After a failed Greif Interdict check on field works or a reserve marker the unit kept its full movement. | It may only stay or move one area. |
| 17.21 | Medium | An Allied air mission was refused for unsupplied units. | Allowed (the rule makes it the exception to 9.34). |
| 16.11 | Medium | Von der Heydte was not treated as a defended roadblock for asset limits and 18.4; a completely scattered drop still stopped the US unit. | Treated like a defended roadblock; after a scattered drop the unit may move on. |
| 18.2 | Medium | Bid fuel assets came back through the alternate event use, and a strat-move spent a regular fuel asset before a bid one. | Bid fuel never returns and is spent first, to the Eliminated Box. |
| 18.3 / 7.31 | Medium | KG Peiper's full exploitation move could cross a blown bridge. | It cannot. |

### Sicily

| Rule | Severity | What was wrong | What the game does now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14.27b | High | When a beach assault was aborted every landing unit lost an extra step before going back to sea. | The units fall back to their landing box and to sea; only the hits actually taken count. |
| 14.27a | Medium | An airborne drop into an already contested area did not make that battle mandatory, and the attack could be aborted once the paratroopers had died. | The battle is mandatory and fought first; an airborne assault can never be aborted. |
| 6.22 / 6.23 | Medium | US and British units in one battle were treated as one Higher Echelon (two artillery assets and battle assets allowed). | Mixed armies: no battle assets, one artillery asset per army (defender: one in total). |
| 17.22 | Medium | A flanking invasion on defenders was not obliged to fight its round in the Admin Phase, and the flank asset could attack in a later Admin Phase. | The round must be fought before the phase ends; after that the asset only defends. |
| 16.32 | Medium | A side that had committed air support could also dogfight the enemy's. | One air commitment per side. |
| 16.34 | Medium | Air supply removed the unsupplied marker (so the units fired at full strength in the enemy turn), skipped the disorder adjustment, and only the phasing player could fly it. | The marker stays; the units skip the morale check; disordered units adjust; either player may fly it. |
| 14.43 | Medium | A retreat crossed an army boundary even when a path inside the unit's own zone existed through a full area. | Retreats inside the own zone are tried first. |
| 14.22 | Medium | A beachhead lost in the Axis half of a turn was destroyed only after the next arrivals had been placed on it. | It is destroyed before the arrivals. |

### Golan '73

| Rule | Severity | What was wrong | What the game does now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17.33 | High | Combat could start without the defender's roll for Electronic Warfare jamming. | Combat cannot begin until the defender has rolled (with its own EW card for +2 when it has one). |
| 4.35 / 16.24 / 16.11a / 17.23 | High | A lone garrison, commando raid or Lt. Zvika got full support: battle assets, air, two artillery assets, events. | One artillery asset only, no events (Zvika may still be reinforced by Special Action, 17.23). |
| 16.24 | High | An isolated garrison never rolled the unsupplied morale check, so it could never die of isolation and kept its area Israeli. | It rolls like a unit and is eliminated on a failure. |
| 22.17 | High | The two free Syrian Breakthrough combat rounds of turn 1 could not be reached from the table (the phase was skipped). | The table offers them. |
| 14.03 | Medium | Israeli units could move to or from Mt Hermon on the night turn. | Nobody can. |
| 14.41 | Medium | Crossing a river on a field connection (no bridge there) triggered a bridge-blowing attempt and could leave a phantom blown bridge that blocked supply. | No attempt where there is no bridge. |
| 16.11a | Medium | After a failed commando raid the Israeli unit could not continue moving. | It may move on with its remaining points. |
| 16.12 | Medium | A SAM answer left pending at the end of the Admin Phase was never resolved: all combat was blocked and Syria could fire the SAM in any later phase. After a SAM cancelled an Admin-Phase mission, Israel could fly another over the same area. | The Admin Phase cannot end with the answer pending; no replacement mission over that area. |
| 17.33 | Medium | With every defender jammed, Syria could still fire a SAM at the Israeli air asset. | No SAM from a jammed defence. |
| 15.7 / 6.8 | Medium | An area held only by a Ghost gave the attacker an exploitation opportunity. | The Ghost is removed, no exploitation. |
| 15.3 | Medium | After the air assault landed in a contested area, Syria could fight other battles first. | The landing is fought first. |
| 15.12 | Medium | With mixed Syrian divisions the air asset could still give +1 battle support. | Air support fires as artillery only. |
| 6.33 / 14.51b | Low | One engineer both removed the field works and bridged the AT ditch in the same round. | One engineer, one job. |

### Interface

| Where | Severity | What was wrong | Fixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combat Phase (all volumes; seen in Golan turn 1) | High | The red button offered "Fight" for a contested area where the side had no attacker in good order (only a strongpoint); the engine refused with "no attacker in good order", and the phase was not skipped. | The button only offers areas with an attacker in good order; the phase is skipped when there is none. |

Checked and found working in the browser: the game loop through the red button for all three volumes (two full turns each), battles to the end with step losses, retreat and exploitation, undo after a move and after a card (and locked after dice), tapping a block then a green area, card plays for every chit kind, the sandbox tools, the phone layout. No JavaScript errors in any run.

## 3. Reported but left as it is

Checked and judged correct, or a reading of the rule the code already follows:

- **Bulge Bodenplatte (17.14)**: the rulebook's table contradicts itself; the engine follows the Player Aid (1–5 no effect, 6–9 delayed, 10 eliminated).
- **Admin Phase order (4.2b/c)**: both players act in any order; enforcing "phasing player first" would change the online flow. Not changed.
- **Delayed reinforcements (4.1c)** may enter at any appropriate entry area, not only the nearest.
- **Sicily flanking invasion in the Axis Admin Phase (16.23)** is not possible; only in the Allied one.
- **Air-supplied areas (Bulge 9.34d)**: Special Actions, staff functions and events still work on them.
- Small text and corner items: the replacement point unit is chosen by the engine; exploitation is declined for all units at once; a Greif bridge is not removed by a strat-move crossing; a US engineer can build field works for a British unit; Rebuild or Replace ignores stacking; the Moroccans can be the sole attacker of a battle (they roll no dice); Golan 14.42 "must leave the sector" is not enforced.
