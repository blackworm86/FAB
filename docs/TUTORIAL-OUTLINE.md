# New lessons: outlines for the owner's approval

Written 14 November 2026 by the project-management session, from the owner's brief: start from each game's Play Book examples, make the lessons fast and bite-sized like a modern video-game tutorial (the old courses took about 50 to 95 minutes), and teach Sicily and Golan '73 only what is new compared with The Bulge. **Nothing is built until the owner approves this outline** (or changes it).

## The shape of every lesson

- **Do first, read after.** The lesson opens on the map with something to tap. A card is one or two short lines (about 140 characters, readable on a phone) and asks for one action. The rule's paragraph is folded under a *why* link for those who want it.
- **2 to 3 minutes, at most 8 steps.** Each lesson ends with what the player can now do ("You can fight a battle"), not a recap list.
- **The book's position and the book's dice.** A lesson that plays an Example of Play starts from the book's position, and the dice come out as the book says, so the screen and the page agree.
- **Never stuck.** Every step has *Show me* (the coach plays it) and every lesson has *Skip lesson*. A dice result waits for Continue, which the lesson does not block.
- **Only what you need now.** Anything a player can learn the first time they meet it in a real game is left out of the course (see "Left out" under each course).
- **The last lesson starts a real game,** the scenario the book recommends for a first game.

Timing is estimated at 10 to 15 seconds a step (read two lines, tap once); each course will be timed by a person before it ships.

## The Bulge: the basics of the series (8 lessons, about 18 minutes)

For a player who has never played Fast Action Battles. Built on Play Book section 23 (Examples of Play: a whole turn) and the Introductory Scenario (24.3).

| # | Lesson | min | What the player does | From the book | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your first move** | 1 | Taps the 26th VG at Prüm, taps a green area: it moves. Nothing else. | 23.3 | 5.1 |
| 2 | **What a block tells you, what a move costs** | 3 | Reads the strength pips and quality on the selected block; moves by road (1 MP) and field (2 MP), enters an occupied area (+1), crosses into another army's zone (+2) and sees the move stop; uses Undo once; one card: German armour needs fuel beyond 3 MP. | 23.3 (26 VG, 116 Pz to St. Vith) | 5.3, 16.13 |
| 3 | **Your first battle** | 3 | Fights St. Vith: picks the point unit, rolls the artillery, then the ground fire against the Success Number, chooses Take losses or Retreat, closes the battle. The face-down blocks turn up when the battle starts. | 23.5 (106th Inf at St. Vith) | 6.2–6.6 |
| 4 | **Chits: your hand of assets** | 2.5 | Draws in the Admin Phase, sees the hand, plays an artillery asset into a battle, watches it go to the Used Box. One card each for: events, the reusable Special Action. | 23.1, 23.2 | 4, 6.22, 8.1 |
| 5 | **Breaking through** | 2.5 | Wins a battle cleanly, rolls the exploitation check, then moves the exploiting armour in the Breakthrough Phase. | 23.6 (Weiswampach), 23.7 | 6.8, 7.3, 7.4 |
| 6 | **Reserves and the long road** | 2 | Marks a block in reserve instead of moving it; later moves a reserve by strategic movement along the roads. | 23.4 (82nd and 101st Airborne) | 5.21, 5.4 |

## What changes compared with today

- **Five courses become three.** Today: The Bulge, Golan (full), Golan "what's new", Sicily (full), Sicily "what's new". Proposed: The Bulge basics, Golan "what's new", Sicily "what's new". A newcomer who opens Golan or Sicily first is sent to the basics, which are the same series rules on The Bulge's map.
- **About 42 minutes for all three**, against about 50 to 95 minutes for each course today.
- **"Left out" topics** need a small new feature: a one-line hint the first time the player meets them in a real game (for example the first time a Greif team or a minefield appears), switchable off in Settings. It is optional; without it, those topics are reached through the rules reader as today. To be designed with a mockup if the owner wants it.
- **Two playtest findings are answered by the new Sicily course**: the unmoved landing blocks going back to sea (lesson 1) and the airborne battle that must come first (lesson 2).

## How it would be built (after approval)

1. The lesson writer (`fab-lesson-writer`) writes the English cards from this outline, The Bulge first; the owner plays the first two lessons on his phone before the rest is written.
2. The translator does the Italian.
3. The lesson walker (`node web/e2e/tutorials.mjs`) walks every course to its end, and a person times each one; any lesson over 3 minutes is cut.
4. The old courses are removed once the new ones pass.

## Progress

- 14 November, first try: The Bulge lessons 1–3 built. The owner's verdict: the wording was too dry and impersonal, it did not explain the mechanics, the course started without saying what the player is fighting for, and the order of the lessons was not convincing.
- 14 November, revised and approved: open with the goal; the lessons follow the order of one German turn, as the Play Book's section 23 tells it; a coach's voice that says what happens, why, and what it means for your plan. Fight (23.5, St. Vith) · 6. Win a battle: artillery and exploitation (23.6, Weiswampach) · 7. Break through (23.7) · 8. Supply (23.8, KG Peiper) · 9. The end of the day: the victory check and the first game (23.10).
  - Every step is played by the tests, the claims on the cards were checked against the engine, the course was walked in a browser (59 steps, every target found) and photographed on a phone.
