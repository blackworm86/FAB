# Golan '73 Play Book: examples of play as test cases

Transcribed (OCR plus reading the scan) from `FAB-G_PlayBook_scan.pdf` (23.0, pages 6–20). Each line is a fact the engine must reproduce; SN is the success number before the roll ("n tries for an s": n dice, a hit on s or less). The map facts are checked by `tools/golan73/check_playbook.py`.

## Reinforcement and Admin
- **Ex 1 Draw of assets (T4).** Syrian Reinforcement Phase: the Reusable Special Action is already in the Available Box, so step 1 lets him retrieve one used asset (Air Support) and step 2 does not apply. Step 3: one die per SAM in the box; 6 or more eliminates one SAM (rolls 8 and 4: one SAM eliminated). Step 4: remaining used assets to the cup; step 5: T4 arrivals to the cup. Admin draws on T4: Syrian turn, Syria 6 and Israel 3; Israeli turn, Israel 4 and Syria 2.
- **Ex 2 Air interdiction and SAM.** Israeli Air Support for interdiction (Admin) in 75 and 73: +1 MP to enter and +1 MP to leave for Syrian units. Syria answers with SAM, one die each on the Air Defense Fire Table, T4–6 column: 3 → air asset to Used Box (SAM too); 6 → no effect (SAM to Used). Move costs: 91st Tank 75→74 road 1, 74→73 road 1+1 = 2, leave 73 = 2; 58th Mech leaves 73 for 3 (occupied +1).

## Movement
- **Ex 3 Basic movement.** Armor max 6 MP, infantry 3. 4th Mech 6→7 road/bridge 1, 7→36 escarpment by road 1+1, 36→37 wadi by field 3: total 6. 6→9 field +1 occupied = 3. 9→8 river with no road: not allowed (no bridge). A field river crossing costs the whole MA. An escarpment boundary: road only, +1 MP, one unit per Movement Phase. Golani: 35→8 escarpment without road: no; 35→36 field 2; then 42 occupied (+1) or 43 wadi (+1): not enough MP; 35→34 road +1 occupied = 2, →38 road 1. 53/188 34→33 road +1 enemy = 2 and stops.
- **Ex 4 Rugged.** Infantry class only, all MP; armor only across a road (18/19, one unit per phase). 121st Mech 50→51→52 road 1+1, 52→46 field +1 = 3; 51→46 rugged: armor may not. 85th Inf 49→46 rugged: all MA. 85th may not cross the operational boundary 49/48 before T5.
- **Ex 5 AT ditch.** Crossing costs all MA (allowed even above the MA), +1 occupied; it is a Crossing Assault (River Assault marker). 64→50, 61→57, 60→59 allowed; 62 (Horfa) cannot reach the ditch and cross in one phase.
- **Ex 6 Minefields.** Crossing a mined boundary: morale check (fail: may not cross; a 7 fails); success costs +1 MP (52nd 48→47: road 1 +1 occupied +1 mine = 3). Failure still spends the MP of the attempt (errata: field 2 +1). 9th Recon detour 48→40→41→47 = 1+2+2 = 5.
- **Ex 7 Strategic movement.** Road connections only, friendly-controlled areas only. 4th Mech from Assembly A with a reserve marker strat-moves to 30; the marker returns to the holding box. Operational alternatives: A→10→11→12 (1 each), 12→24 road + escarpment 2, 24→26 1; or 12→35→34→38.

## Combat
- **Ex 8 Basic combat (38).** Attacker may commit up to 8 battle assets, Defender 5. Israeli: Armor Task Force + 405th+55th artillery; Syrian: 89th artillery + Air Support. Artillery: 405th+55th SN 5−1 (armor point) −1 (target defender) = 3, rolls 2: hit (first priority: field works removed). 89th SN 5−1 = 4, rolls 7: miss. Syrian ground fire, 91st Tank: +1 armor, +1 ground support, −1 armor point, +1 elite on veteran = 7, three dice 2, 3, 8: two hits. Israel loses the Task Force (mandatory step loss), then a 4th Mech step (it does not abort). Israeli ground fire, 4th Mech brigade integrity (both units present: one extra die): +1 armor, −1 armor point, +0 TDN, −1 veteran on elite = 4, three dice 4, 5, 9: one hit; second 4th Mech same SN, 1, 6, 8: one hit. Syria: Air Support is not a battle asset; 91st retreats to 30 disordered (first hit), second hit is a step loss.
- **Ex 9 Crossing assault (27, 28).** Strongpoints fire 1 die whatever their steps, defend only, never retreat, can be evacuated. Artillery on a strongpoint: 5 −1 strongpoint −1 defender = 3. Hit on a strongpoint: disordered (second priority). Strongpoint ground fire: infantry-supported −1, armor-supported +1, +1 vs a River Assault, quality ±, disordered −1 (Tel Saki: 5−1+1+1+1 = 7). Syrian fire in a crossing assault −1; vs infantry-supported strongpoint +1? (72nd Inf: +1 ground support, −1 crossing, +1 = 6, four dice, two hits). Armor-supported strongpoint target +1. A 2-step strongpoint with two hits is eliminated. An engineer asset that survived and did not fire bridges one AT ditch boundary of the area (either one, the road one chosen): it becomes an open boundary with its connection.
- **Ex 10 Air assault.** Only the Syrian 82nd Para, once, east of the SAM barrier or into Mt Hermon; resolved before any other combat; River Assault marker; not on T2 (night).
- **Ex 11 EW.** Played as the one event per side in a battle. Defender rolls a die (+2 if he plays his own EW); 2+2 = 4 disorders the Golani; with all his units disordered he may assign no artillery, air, SAM or battle assets.
- **Ex 12 Counterbattery.** Played out of sequence after the enemy commits artillery; success on 1–5; the enemy artillery goes on the Turn Track three turns later.

## Other phases
- **Ex 13 Commando raid.** In the Admin Phase a commando goes to a vacant area adjacent to a Syrian unit. An Israeli unit entering pays +1 MP (24: road 1 + escarpment 1 + raid 1 = 3) and triggers the raid roll (2: success); the raid fights in the Combat Phase as a defended roadblock. Units may not strat-move or retreat into a raided area.
- **Ex 14 Reaction and Breakthrough.** Non-phasing reserve units may move fully (into contested areas, never creating new ones). Breakthrough: reserve units move fully, exploitation-marked armor or a Special Action moves one area; new contests allowed and their combat is mandatory. 317th 26→31 field +1 occupied = 3.
- **Ex 15 Supply.** Trace across an unbridged AT ditch only if the unit is adjacent to it (14.93), across a bridged one freely (14.94); across an escarpment only by road; blocked by minefields and enemy strongpoints; may pass through occupied friendly areas and out of an occupied enemy-controlled area.
