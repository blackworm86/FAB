# Playtest 2, 14 November 2026

Second run of the playtester helpers, after the interface session worked through the first report. Four runs: The Bulge on a phone (the early phases the first test never reached), Golan '73 on desktop (Electronic Warfare in play), Sicily on a phone (airborne rule, blocks left at sea), and the three new lessons in Italian on a phone. Each capped at 45 minutes. First report: `docs/playtest-reports/2026-11-13.md` (its letters A, B, C are used below).

## In one paragraph

**The fixes from the first report held.** Of the first test's problems that were re-checked, the main ones are gone: the phone turn pill and instruction lines are whole, the Golan top bar no longer overlaps, "End anyway?" now waits, Sicily's "Begin combat" says why it is waiting, Golan's set-up says why "End set-up" waits, the hit-choice box and the battle-over panel are readable. **One new real bug** was found and fixed the same day: in Italian, Bulge lesson 2 could not be finished. What remains is smaller: a few things on the phone and in Golan's Electronic Warfare screens that are unclear rather than wrong. Nothing crashed; no browser error in any of the four runs.

How it was checked: I opened the pictures for the findings marked ✔; the rest are the helpers' reports. Pictures that are kept are beside this file.

## B. New findings, worst first

### Fixed already
1. ✔ **Italian only: Bulge lesson 2 could not be finished** (`lessons-it-replacement-card-stuck.jpg`). The "Fresh troops" step waits for the replacement card to be selected and looked for an English word on the screen; in Italian the card reads differently, so tapping it did nothing. Fixed and pushed on 14 November; tested in Italian in a browser.
2. **Italian: the pop-up for tapping the wrong thing was garbled** ("Tocca 12 VG block. illuminato"). The hint is now translated before the full stop is added. (Fixed, but I could not make the pop-up appear in my own test, so I have not seen the fixed version.)

### Golan: Electronic Warfare (played through the screen for the first time)
3. ✔ **The jamming roll reads the wrong way round** (`golan-jam-dice-pass-but-disordered.jpg`). The battle-over list shows "Veteran Armor Strongpoint: jamming, SN 5, die 5, **pass**" in green, yet the unit was disordered. "Pass" and the colours are from the jammer's side; the Israeli player reads them the other way. With Countermeasures the list shows "die 10, fail" although the unit held, and nothing says that 10 is 8 plus 2.
4. **The jam roll is not shown when it happens.** After "Roll without countermeasures" (or the Countermeasures card) the panel just disappears and a D appears on the block; the dice only show at "Battle over". (The first report's A8 is half fixed; the SAM roll was not reached.)
5. **While jammed, the instruction still says "click a card to add it"** and offers hand cards. Tapping one gives a good message ("all defending units are jammed: no artillery, air, SAM or battle assets (17.33)"), so the rule works; the instruction line should say it up front.
6. **Counterbattery gives no feedback when it is wasted** (no artillery fired: a ghost defender is removed at "Begin combat" with no roll). The card disappears; nothing says it was spent for nothing.
7. **The EW and Counterbattery answer cards are not visible until the moment** (acceptable once you know the rules, unclear to a newcomer).
8. **Assets placed in a battle show as blank grey squares with an X** with no hint that this is hidden information on purpose.

### The Bulge, phone
9. **The Engineer chit in Admin could not be used** (`bulge-engineer-no-glowing-area.jpg`). The line says "Tap a glowing area where the engineer works (its own army zone)", but no area visibly glows: the chit panel and the hand chip cover about half the map, and taps on two areas were refused with no message. Possibly stuck for a player; needs a look by the interface session.
10. **Disabled buttons give no reason** (the Reserve button greyed out on the block panel); the movement-point bubbles with a droplet (fuel) are never explained.
11. **The CAN MOVE / FORTIFY / RESERVE chips act as "pick the next block" and nothing says so**; the counts change without a visible cause.
12. **A block seems to sit far from its area label in the tilted 3D view**, and after Undo the block still looked as if it were in the old area (the counters had returned). Unconfirmed; may be perspective.
13. **Area names vanish when the camera is zoomed out**, and come back when it moves in; a newcomer may think the labels are broken.
14. The turn pill says "T2 · GE ADMIN" while the Allied side acts in the Admin step; the View panel says "Map: 2D" is selected but describes the relief.

### Sicily, phone
15. ✔ **The drop-zone panel covers the zone it asks you to tap** (`sicily-drop-zone-under-panel.jpg`). The line says "tap the drop zone, or tap elsewhere to cancel"; the lit zone is under the panel, and the first tap did nothing. It only worked after tapping Hide, which no text mentions.
16. **For a unit with no legal drop zone the line still says "choose the drop zone in the panel"**, while the panel offers only "Remove the unit"; the unit is lost and the Axis scores 1 point, unexplained on that screen.
17. **A "Deselect" button stays on the map after a move** with nothing selected; **toasts stack over the blocks and destinations** you need next; **the camera pans after a tap**, so a second tap aimed at the same screen point can land on an enemy block and open its card.

### Golan, desktop
18. ✔ **The hand overflows the screen** (`golan-hand-overflows-edges.jpg`): the first and last cards are cut at the edges and the EW card sticks out over the bottom edge (the first report's C10, still there).
19. Small: "hit on 0 or less" for a Success Number of 0 (impossible to hit; reads like a typo); "brass = costs a Special Action" and "NO SPECIAL ACTION LEFT" labels are jargon; block faces went blank for a moment after the Syrian breakthrough (could not be reproduced); the sandbox cannot be opened while a battle is open and its search for "electronic" finds nothing.

### The new lessons, in Italian
20. The Italian reads naturally in the cards the helper read, with the game's own terms and the glossary; the opening cards of Bulge and Golan fit on the phone. Lessons 1–5 of the Bulge, 1–3 of Golan and 1–3 of Sicily were reached; the helper had to use the coach's own shortcut to pass a few steps, which hides problems (so the verdicts are provisional).
21. **The coach card covers the panel it is explaining** in five places (Bulge "Chi sta davanti" with the gold point-unit marker, "Quale ponte"; Golan "A faccia in su" and "Sopra Jasim"; Sicily "Dove può atterrare"): the card sits over the top half of the screen where the panel's header or options are.
22. **Sicily lesson 1, "Al largo": a tap on the ring did nothing** (the ring sat on the Joss area, not on the block, and a toast covered the block). Partly the script's timing; the toast covering the block is real. Lesson 2 steps "Lancio" and "Ora le altre" also did not respond to the helper's taps; not diagnosed.
23. A pile of overlapping movement-point labels crowds the target in Golan lesson 1 ("Il giro lungo").
24. The Golan opening card has no visible "learn the basics first" link, which the outline promised.
25. The word "pedina" is used for a chit while the game also says "blocco" for a unit: consistent, but a newcomer may confuse them.

## C. For the rules session (questions, not rulings)

1. **Golan:** after a jam, a strongpoint (a standing asset) rolled for jamming and became disordered; the rule says "not garrisons or other standing assets". Is a strongpoint a "defending unit" for 17.33?
2. **Golan:** a "SN 0" fires dice and one die showed 4: if the number is really 0 nothing can hit; do the display and the engine agree?
3. **Golan:** are EW and Counterbattery chits eliminated after use, or "used"? (They were eliminated.) And a ghost defender is removed at "Begin combat" with no roll (first report's D6, still open).
4. **Sicily:** after the 82nd Airborne dropped, "Comiso" never appeared as a battle. Is a drop into an empty area meant to need no battle? Is losing an airborne unit with no drop zone worth +1 to the Axis (14.27a)? Do beachheads lost by leaving blocks unmoved give the Axis points (14.24b)? The track went AX +8 to +9 with no on-screen reason.
5. **Sicily:** field works absorbed one of three hits in a river assault where the garrison then retreated: allowed?
6. **The Bulge:** which areas can an Engineer asset legally work in (finding 9)? Why is Reserve disabled for some blocks (what decides it)?

## D. What was not assessed

- **The Bulge:** no artillery committed, no roll, no hit choice or Retreat, no Allied battle, no event chit (the hand had three Engineer chits). The helper reached Combat only.
- **Sicily:** no Axis attack, no dogfight, whether other battles are blocked before the airborne one (the button was greyed; nobody tapped another red area), an untried garrison that fails its morale check (the one roll passed).
- **Golan:** no retreat, no first-hit step, no SAM roll, no Israeli attack, no phone run.
- **The lessons:** Bulge 6–9, Golan 4–6, Sicily 4–7 and every closing card were not reached; the minutes per lesson could not be measured (three browsers shared one machine).
- Online play, the 3D figures, tablets and landscape were not part of this run.

1. **Interface session:** items 9 (Engineer chit), 15 (drop-zone panel), 18 (hand overflow), 3–5 (Golan jam screens), 21 (lesson card over the panel it explains), then A7 (say it on the button) and the smaller items.

## F. How the helpers did

Four runs; three hit their call limit and had to be asked for a report (they now report from what they have). About 60 findings; 7 pictures opened by me, all held up. The most useful result was the lessons run, which found a bug that no automatic check catches (an English word hidden in a lesson's "done" test). The least useful part was the Bulge phone run, which spent most of its calls on one stuck chit and never reached a battle. Next time: one narrow goal per helper (for example "play a retreat and a Close battle", not a whole turn).
