# Playtest, 13 November 2026

First run of the playtester helpers. Six play-throughs, each a helper playing through the screen on the development server: The Bulge, Golan '73 and Sicily, desktop (1380x860) and phone (iPhone 13). Turn 1 of both sides, every battle offered fought to Close battle. About 20 to 75 minutes each (the machine was slow: a screenshot took 8 to 60 seconds).

Code at the time: `main` after the 13 November splits and the "no automation" change.

## Result in one paragraph

**Nothing crashed and nothing in the game state broke.** No browser error in any of the six runs, every phase was reached, 30 battles were fought to the end. The problems are all in what the player sees: the phone cuts off the phase name and half the instructions, some panels cover the thing you need next, a few messages are unclear, and one place in Sicily can look stuck. Nothing found says The Bulge is broken, but The Bulge was played far less than it needs to be before a release (see "not assessed").

How the findings were checked: the project manager read the helpers' reports, opened the pictures for the findings marked ✔ and saw the problem. The others are the helpers' reports, with a picture they took but that was not re-opened. Pictures marked ✔ are kept beside this file.

## D. For the rules session (questions the helpers could not settle, not rulings)

1. **Bulge, Allied Combat phone run:** after closing one battle the game went straight to Reaction while the panel still said "6 battles" were left. Intended, or does the Allied side get more battles? (Possibly a leftover count from the German turn; nobody has checked.)
2. **Bulge:** the same battle at Udenbreth showed SN 4 in one run and SN 3 in another for 277 VG (and 5/4 for 12 VG). Different cards or field works may explain it.
3. **Bulge:** after an Allied "Take losses" the Battle over list shows only German losses.
4. **Golan:** the sandbox goes from set-up straight into a Syrian Combat phase on turn 1, with no Syrian reinforcement, admin or movement. Right for the scenario?
5. **Golan:** SAM roll: a 5 against SN 0 counted as a hit.
6. **Golan:** ghosts and unrevealed defenders are eliminated without a roll once revealed.
7. **Sicily:** an untried garrison revealed with a 7 against SN 3 counts as a pass (15.3b).
8. **Sicily:** the 82nd Airborne was eliminated for lack of a drop zone on turn 1; the Axis got "+1 VP" for beachheads destroyed because blocks were left unmoved; four airborne blocks are listed but only two drop panels appeared (US10 and BR12 have no area).
9. **Sicily:** Pachino is offered as "next battle" while the airborne battle at Comiso must be fought first (14.27a): intended order? (This is A1.)
10. The Golan Electronic Warfare questions from the referee's reading (see `docs/STATUS.md`).

## E. What was not assessed (so "no finding" is not "fine")

- **The Bulge was thin:** the sandbox opens it in Combat, so German Admin, Movement and Strategic were not played; no card or asset was played on the Allied side; no Retreat choice was taken; the Pieces window, the Map menu and the legend were never opened.
- **No retreat** was fought in Golan or Sicily; **no Axis battle** in Sicily; **Golan's Electronic Warfare** did not come up; Israeli-side events were not played.
- **Sizes:** font sizes were measured only for the "−1" labels; sideways scrolling and pinch zoom were not tested by hand; landscape and tablet not tested.
- **Dice animation and 3D look:** screenshots were too slow to judge.
- **Online play, the Italian interface, the lessons, 3D figures and the 3D map** were not part of this run.
- The sandbox rolls real random dice, so no two runs are the same; nothing was repeated to confirm a finding.

| Value | the phone problems (B1, B2, B3, B4) and A1, A3, A4, A6 are things none of the checks catch and that the owner would have met on his phone |

Verdict (the project manager's, for the owner to overrule): worth it for the phone and for first contact with a flow that changed; not a substitute for the owner's own play, and thin on The Bulge's early phases. Next time: brief them to start The Bulge from Admin (not from the sandbox's Combat), cap each run at about 45 minutes, and ask each to stop and report at its turn limit.

## G. Suggested order for the interface session

1. B1, B2: the turn pill and the instruction line on the phone (they touch everything else the player reads).
2. A1, A2: Sicily's "Begin combat" and "Assets done" must say why they do nothing.
3. A3: the top bar at desktop width.
4. A4: the upside-down name on blocks that lost a step.
5. A6: "End anyway?": decide (owner) whether it should wait or fall back; if it falls back, say so on the button.
6. C1, B3, B4: panels and pop-ups covering what they describe.
7. The rest, by how often a player meets them.

A5, A7, A8, A9 and the questions in D go to the rules session or the owner first: they may be the rules, not the table.
