# Playtest 3, 14 November 2026 (evening)

Third run of the playtester helpers, after the interface session built the eight design-review fixes and the Sicily fixes. Four narrow runs, each about 40 minutes: The Bulge on desktop (artillery, a retreat, the Allied side's choices, the rest of the turn), Sicily on desktop (the sea boxes, a garrison battle, the zones layer), Golan '73 on a phone (its first ever phone run) and The Bulge on a phone (battles and the Map layers panel). Pictures I opened myself are marked ✔ and kept in `2026-11-14b/`.

## In one paragraph

**The new bottom bar works and the game played through.** The Bulge went through a whole German turn (eight battles, breakthrough, supply) to the Allied turn with no browser error in any run; artillery fired, a retreat and a Take losses were played from the bar; the Sicily garrison now stands face up after its battle; the army zones layer and the rebuilt Map layers panel work. **Nothing was stuck.** What remains is a short list: one stale instruction in Golan, hidden cards visible before the hand-over, the "who decides" tag wrong while reading the other side's dice, the result list cut by the bottom bar on the phone, and Sicily's At Sea box drawn as an unreadable heap and left off screen.

## Findings, worst first

### Wrong
1. ✔ **Golan: a stale "Air Mission +2: tap one of your blocks" line** stays through the rest of the battle and into the next one, while the red button says "Roll the dice" (`golan-phone-stale-air-mission.jpg`).
2. ✔ **Golan: the defender's hand is face up before the hand-over.** While "Pass to the Israeli player · tap to start" is showing, the Israeli cards are readable by the Syrian player (`golan-phone-hand-visible-before-pass.jpg`). In a one-browser game the pass strip is the only privacy there is, so the hand should stay face down until the tap. (Online, each seat has its own screen; not checked.)
3. ✔ **"Who decides" is wrong while reading dice.** The Allied defensive-fire result is shown under "German decides"; elsewhere "Allied decides" appears after the German roll. The tag follows whoever presses Continue, not whose dice they are (`bulge-desktop-german-decides-allied-dice.jpg`). Seen on The Bulge desktop and phone.
4. ✔ **Phone (dev links only): the sandbox button sits on the Supply tile** of the Map layers panel; tapping Supply opens the sandbox drawer instead (`bulge-phone-sandbox-over-supply.jpg`). Players on the real site do not have the sandbox button, but the owner's test links do.

### Unreadable
5. ✔ **The "Battle over" and dice lists are cut by the bottom bar on the phone**, half a row of dice sliced, no sign that more is below (`golan-phone-dice-cut.jpg`, `bulge-phone-result-cut.jpg`). Golan and The Bulge.
6. ✔ **Sicily: the At Sea box is a heap.** About 15 blocks piled in one stack, spilling past the box edges; you cannot tell them apart (`sicily-desktop-at-sea-heap.jpg`).
7. ✔ **Sicily: the camera leaves out the west of the map** after set-up (At Sea, Airborne Assault, the D boxes, Palermo), and "Reset view" does not bring them back (`sicily-desktop-west-off-screen.jpg`).

### Unclear
8. ✔ **Golan: two red buttons compete**: "Choose the landing area" (an Israeli action) beside "Next battle: Tel Saki" under "Syrian decides" (`golan-phone-two-red-buttons.jpg`).
9. ✔ **"Hit on 3–4 or less"** when two units with Success Numbers 4 and 3 fire together: reads as "3 or 4" (`bulge-desktop-hit-on-3-4.jpg`). Better: one line per unit, or "hit on 4 or less (12 VG), 3 or less (277 VG)".
10. **The layout jumps** when the pass strip goes away (Golan phone): a tap aimed at a card hit Rules.
11. **Small**: card labels in a Golan battle on the phone about 6–8 px; minefield messages cover the map in Golan set-up; "Shuffle face-down" places all eleven pieces with no word saying so; Sicily's lit move destination is a pale tan, easy to miss; the pass strip covers the bottom of the battle set-up while "Begin combat" is the button; the untried garrison's reveal roll is not explained at that moment; "Allied hand · 0" chip floats over the map in Admin; the point-unit pointer sits on "Choose in the bar below".

### Works (seen in pictures)
- ✔ The Sicily garrison revealed in battle stands face up afterwards, with label, disorder marker and pips (`sicily-desktop-garrison-face-up.jpg`).
- ✔ The rebuilt Map layers panel: equal tiles, one On / Dim / Off row, Legend / Rotate / Reset view (`bulge-phone-sandbox-over-supply.jpg`, apart from the sandbox button).
- Take losses / Retreat in the bar with their explanations ("1 hit fewer · 3 areas to choose"); every bar button is 44 px or more; "Pass to the player" strips in the side's colour; "End anyway? 1 can move" stays armed; the run of empty steps passed with "End combat · go to breakthrough".

## For the rules session (questions, not findings)
1. **The Bulge, Admin of the Allied player turn:** the button reads "Done · German acts" (4.2). Is the non-phasing player's Admin second, so this is right?
2. **The Bulge:** the same artillery card (405th) appeared in battle 2 after battle 1 in the same turn: can one artillery asset fight in two battles of the same Combat Phase?
3. **Golan:** one Syrian artillery hit removed a ghost point unit and ended the battle with no Israeli fire (Tel Saki).
4. **Sicily:** the untried garrison's reveal check (15.3b) comes before defensive fire, SN 3 failing on 5 or more: right? Airborne units with no drop zone are eliminated at the end of movement: right (14.27a)?

## Not assessed
- **The retreat screen with several areas to choose**: every retreat had one place to go, so the framing and naming fix is untested.
- **Releasing a division by name** (no Allied Admin reached in Sicily); an Axis attack in Sicily; Golan's Electronic Warfare, a retreat, an Israeli attack; The Bulge's Admin and Movement on the phone (the sandbox opens in Combat); the welcome card; online play.
- One helper restarted every browser on the machine once; the Bulge desktop run had to replay its first pass.

## How the helpers did
Four runs, about 40 minutes each, about 35 findings and 7 rule questions; 11 pictures opened by me, all held up. Narrow goals worked better than whole turns (each run reached its goal or said why not). Next time: one helper for the retreat screen with several areas and for releasing a division, with a set-up that makes them happen.
