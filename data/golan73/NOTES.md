# Golan '73 units and counters: open questions

Sources: Vassal module `docs/games/golan73/other/FAB_Golan73.vmod` (buildFile.xml: 254 PieceSlots + 11 CardSlots, block face images, the saves "Operation Badr Historical Setup" and "Battle for Nafakh"), the printed Setup Cards (`IsraeliSetupCard.pdf`, `SyrianSetupCard.pdf`), the Nafakh scenario PDF, the rules (sections 13 to 17) and the errata (9/23/17). The Vassal images are reference only and are not in the repo, so every `image` is `null` and `vassal_image(s)` gives the module file name.

## Choices to confirm

- **15th and 58th Mech, 3 or 4 steps?** The data uses 3 steps (all veteran). The Vassal block (`PROP Steps max 3`, three face images) and the Nafakh setup card both show 3 steps. The 2016 printed Syrian Setup Card shows both blocks with 4 pips on the top edge.
- **Strongpoint `steps` = stars.** Elite armor has 2 stars, veteran armor 3, green and veteran infantry 2 each (taken from the Vassal `Steps` max and the faces). 15.6 calls them one-step units that always fire 1 die. The data therefore also carries `fire_dice: 1`, `vp_steps: 1` (errata: no VP) and `fire_sn_mod` +1 for the tank symbol and -1 for the rifle symbol. `cls` is `infantry` because the errata says strongpoints are fired at as infantry behind field works.
- **Badr strongpoint placement.** The errata's "rough historical" list (59, 57, 50, 49, 48, 40, 30, 30, 29, 29, 27) repeats areas 30 and 29, but the rule is one piece per area. The 11 Purple Line areas run north to south 59, 57, 50, 49, 48, 40, 39, 30, 29, 28, 27 (map.json labels plus the 7 Syrian start areas). Reading the first "30" as 39 and the second "29" as 28 gives the `suggested_historical` list: 39 elite tank, 30 ghost, 29 green rifle, 28 veteran tank. By the rules the Israeli player chooses the placement.
- **Golani 2A in Nafakh.** The Nafakh save releases the block with the "2A" face (I02), so the data uses I02, not I01.
- **Israeli division (18.5) read from the colour of the symbol box.** Olive is 36th: Golani, 188th, 7th, 317th and the strongpoints. Beige/pink is 240th: 179th, 679th, 4th. Light blue is 146th: 9th, 205th, 670th. The 317th sitting in the 36th is the colour reading only, not checked against history.
- **Syrian divisions from the stripe colour.** Yellow is 5th Inf (61, 112, 46, 132, 5 Recon, and the 47th Tank T2 block also has a yellow stripe). Light blue is 9th Inf (33, 52, 43, 51, 9 Recon). Red is 7th Inf (68, 85, 78, 121, 7 Recon, and Moroc also has a red stripe). Grey-green is 1st Tank, beige is 3rd Tank. The 82nd Para and the SR units have no stripe, so they are independent.
- **SR mech brigade: 62nd or 90th?** 15.2 and 17.11 name the "90th Mech Brigade", but the block, the release event (T8, misspelt "Replaced" in the Vassal slot name) and the setup card all say 62nd. The data uses 62nd.
- **185th Commando.** The Vassal slot name says "85th Commando", but the image and the card say 185th Cdo. The data uses 185th.
- **679th battalion event.** "Battalion only for 679th Arm Bde" (T4) is on the Israeli Setup Card and in the Nafakh save but missing from the Vassal Badr turn track. It is included at T4.
- **Start areas changed by errata:** 77/7th is in 42 (the sticker says 47) and 53/188(-) is in 31 (the sticker and card say 30). Both units carry `sticker_area`. GHQ Artillery is at Start (the module already shows S).
- **Kind naming.** Where the rules match Bulge, the data reuses the Bulge kind: artillery, rocket_artillery, engineer, armor_asset, infantry_asset, air_mission, command_staff, garrison, task_force, special_action_event, event_release, reserve_marker, special_action_block. Everything else gets a new kind: sam, commando, strongpoint, ghost, minefield, replacement_mech, event_ew, event_counterbattery, event_arriving_battalion, event_ssm_strike, event_dugman5, event_lt_zvika, event_king_hassan, marker_control, marker_at_ditch_bridged.
- **Militia, Druze, AT and Sayeret Egoz are `infantry_asset` with a `subtype`** (militia / anti_tank / special_forces), because the base rules give them no special rules (errata: "AT is an infantry class battle asset"). AT has optional rule 18.6 in the Nafakh doc. Make them separate kinds if the engine needs that.
- **Air support is `air_mission` (Bulge art), but in Golan it is an asset for both sides (16.3), not an Allied-only event.** The Bulge engine's `isAsset()` would class it as an event.
- **Arriving battalions use the new kind `event_arriving_battalion` (art `bulge:event_arriving_regiment`).** The rule differs from Bulge: 2 steps, or bring back one eliminated block at 2 steps (17.21). The `brigade` field names the brigade.
- **Replacements may give elite steps (17.31/17.32), flagged `elite_allowed`.** Mech Replacement covers mech units and the infantry class, so it is `replacement_mech` with the replacement_inf art.
- **Chit quality from the pip colour** (white = green, black = veteran, red = elite). Artillery shows no pip, so it has no quality. Like Bulge, artillery carries `class: infantry`.
- **Turn codes:** `S` = start (Available Box), `C` = conditional (3 air support assets go to the Air Support Holding Box, the 8 task forces to the Evacuated Strongpoints box, each with a `conditional` trigger), `scenario` = garrisons. Markers have `turn: null`.
- **Counter backs:** Israeli asset, Syrian asset and the "Strongpoint" back of the task forces come from the Vassal backs. The garrisons' physical back is unknown (Vassal never hides them), so `back_garrison` has no image. These backs are in the new `counter_backs.json`, built the same way as Bulge's.
- **Generic marker counts** (control, river assault, exploitation, disordered, roadblock, blown bridge, field works, AT ditch bridged, out of supply) are unknown, so `copies: null` and `limit: unlimited`. Known counts: minefield 6, Israeli reserve 4, Syrian reserve 2, game turn 1, VPs 1. The 228-counter sheet was not scanned, so these could not be checked.
- **Badr river assault markers: 27, 28, 39, 48, 50, 57, 59** (the errata's correction of the scenario text's 26, 27, 39, 47, 50, 57, 58). The same seven areas start contested.
- **Badr Syrian reserve markers start on 132nd Mech (S15) and 51st Tank (S13)**, per the scenario text quoted in the errata. The setup card says the holding box; the errata says the scenario text overrides it.
- **Badr minefields:** 29/30, 47/48, 50/51 are printed on the map and placed by the Vassal setup. The setup-card pairs 26/83, 51/52, 47/50 are rejected per the errata. The other 3 are placed by the Israeli player.
- **Israeli VPs start at 6** (errata). The card says 5th space.
- **The Golan Play Book's 22.1 (Badr scenario text) is not in docs.** Start phase (combat), "Syrian admin skipped T1" and the 22.17 free breakthrough round come from the errata and the card notes. The Al-Owda scenario (docs/scenarios) was not built.
- **Nafakh reinforcement chits.** The cards print them under "Available Box" brackets, while the Vassal save puts them on the turn track. Do they go straight to Available, or into the cup as usual? Some arrive on a different turn than their counter code (e.g. 183rd Cdo S at T2, 185th Cdo 5 at T3); the data uses the scenario turn.
- **Nafakh 179th, 33rd, 52nd, 43rd start reduced** (save `Steps` state and card). The data gives them as `reduced` or the reinforcement `steps`. The 52nd also starts `recovering`.
- **The Badr save gives no step reductions.** Only the 179th (2/4), 4th (3/4) and 679th (2/3) arrive reduced, from the yellow arrow on the block and the initial `Steps` in Vassal.
- **map.json `assembly.A` is [6, 10].** Rule 14.22 says "area 6 (Gadot) or area 8 (Almagor)", but on the Vassal zones Almagor is 10 and 8 is Had Nes. Worth checking during the map review (map.json was not touched here).
