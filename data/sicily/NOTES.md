# FAB: Sicily data notes

## Units and counters

Built by `tools/sicily/pieces.py` (see `tools/sicily/README.md`). Sources: the Vassal module `docs/games/sicily/other/FAB_Sicily.vmod` (one image per piece; where each piece sits on its three set-up cards; no step or quality data, so pips and codes were read off the images), the owner's scans (`docs/games/sicily/counters`: sticker sheet, counter sheet front and back) and the player aids (`SICILY_PLAYER_AIDS.pdf`: p. 7 Allied reinforcements, p. 10 Allied at start, p. 12 Axis at start and reinforcements, p. 8/9/11 Sunrise Bridge, p. 2 event summary, p. 5 asset matrix, p. 6 fading Italian morale). The Sicily Play Book is not available, so its section numbers below come from the aids only. Every Vassal image is reference only: `image` is `null` and `vassal_image(s)` names the module file.

Counts: 73 blocks (US 12, British 15, Allied Special Action 1; Italian 8 + 29 untried Coastal/Garrison, German 7, Axis Special Action 1) and the full 228-counter sheet in 152 entries (US 46 assets and events, British 33, Allied Fading Italian Morale 3, German 24, Italian 24, markers 98). The script checks each entry against the module (image present, number of pieces per image, turn on the set-up card and in the file name, no module image left over) and the 228 total.

### Model

- **Sides `allied` and `axis`; `nation` us / british / german / italian.** The 1st Canadian Division and 1st Canadian Armoured Brigade are `british` (8th Army, "BR" in Vassal) with `canadian: true` (red unit box). `echelon` is the army: `us_7th_army`, `br_8th_army`, and `it_6th_army` for every Axis piece, German included, because the card is "AT START: Italian 6th Army" and the 6th Army Reserve markers serve both. Nation gives the colour (US olive, British tan, Italian orange, German grey). Allied-wide pieces (Special Action block, Fading Italian Morale) have `nation: null`.
- **Blocks:** `quality_by_step[i]` is the quality at i+1 steps, read edge by edge (white pip green, black veteran, red elite). `division` + `size` (division / brigade / garrison), `colours` (stripe, unit box, sticker paper sampled from the Vassal face), `sticker_code` as printed: `Inv` = lands on turn 1 (`arrival: invasion`), a turn number = reinforcement (`arrival_turn`, `entry: null` = not known), an Axis number in the yellow box = `start_area`, `25R` = area 25 with a 6th Army reserve marker (`start_reserve`), `S*` = untried set-up. No block shows a yellow arrow, so none arrives reduced.
- **Kinds reused from The Bulge / Golan:** artillery, engineer, infantry_asset, armor_asset, air_mission (Air Support, as in Golan), special_action_event, special_action_block, command_staff, replacement_inf, replacement_arm, reserve_marker, marker, marker_control, field_works. **New:** `untried` (block kind), `naval_support`, `event_asset_delayed`, `event_fading_morale`, `event_army_release`, `event_patton_slap`, `event_tactical_extract`, `marker_restricted`, `marker_beachhead`, `marker_port_damage`. `subtype` marks ranger, commando, marines, paratroop, bersaglieri, blackshirt, mobile_group; `flanking_invasion`, `airborne`, `red_flag`, `division` are flags read off the counter.
- **Turn codes:** `S` start (Available Box), number = turn, `C` conditional (`conditional` + `start_box`), markers `null`. `reinforcements.json` lists both sides per turn plus `invasion`, `untried` and `conditional`; `setups.json` has `campaign` and `sunrise_bridge`.
- **Backs:** Allied assets green "Asset or Event" (82), Axis grey (48, Italian included). Two-faced markers carry the other face as `back`: Control (Allied star / Axis cross), Game Turn, VPs, port damage 1/2, River Assault / Road Block, Exploitation / Out of Supply, Disordered / Recovering, Field Works / Blown Bridge, Restricted / Released. Reserve and Beach Head markers print the same face on both sides (`back: "same"`).

### Choices to confirm

- **First player and start phase.** Both set-ups say Allied `first_player` and start in the Movement Phase of turn 1, inferred from "Allied Admin Phase skipped on Game Turn 1" (Axis card), "skip first Admin Phase" (Sunrise Bridge, both sides) and the Allied/Axis order of the turn track. Needs the Play Book.
- **Block wood colours.** The Vassal hidden faces: US dark green (#384b0d), British blue (#384ab7), German brown (#7c6351). Italian blocks hide behind "Axis IT Block Template" (green wood, #6d814e) although their face frames are brown like the Germans'. Which is the physical wood?
- **Panzergrenadier and HG blocks** (X in an oval) are `symbol: mech`, `cls: armor`, as Golan's mech. **Livorno** (X with two dots) is `motorized_inf`, infantry class, `motorized: true` (movement table: "Motorized Units, Livorno Division (see 15.1), 3 or 6"). **Assietta** (X with a triangle at the foot) is read as `mountain_inf`, infantry class; no aid mentions a rule for it.
- **25R and the two 6th Army Reserve markers.** The card shows one marker beside the 25R 15th PG block and says the other goes in the Axis Reserve Holding Box, so the data puts M03a on GE01 and M03b in the box. Vassal starts both markers in the holding box.
- **Untried Coastal/Garrison blocks (15.3): 24 one-step green, 4 two-step green, 1 two-step veteran.** Vassal deck, sticker sheet and card agree. Where they go in the campaign (code S*) is in the Play Book. Vassal has 29 "?" helper markers that are swapped for random garrison blocks; those are not physical pieces and are not in the data. Sunrise Bridge: 7 + 2 + 1 at random face down, one per area in 1-7, 13, 14 and 26.
- **Entry areas of reinforcements** are not on the cards (`entry: null`), except Sunrise Bridge's Axis turn 2 (areas 16, 17 and/or 25) and turn 4 (25 or 26). How the Allied `Inv` blocks land (beaches A1-A4, B1-B6, D1-D13 in the Vassal zones; the Beach Head markers "depending on selected Landing Plan"; the Airborne Assault box for the two airborne divisions?) is in the Play Book.
- **Conditional assets: card or map?** The Allied At Start card says the 3 conditional Air Support go to the "Captured Ports and Key VP Areas box" and the 3 Fading Italian Morale to the "Fading Italian Morale Box". The map prints the Air Support in the **Captured Airfields** box ("As airfields are captured, place one asset into the Allied Selection Cup. The next Axis Air Support Asset that is drawn is placed in the vacated space") and the Fading Italian Morale in **Captured Ports and Key VP Areas** ("Add one to Selection Cup for each undamaged port controlled and for each mutual VP area controlled"); the map's Fading Italian Morale Track (Level 1-3) holds the played ones. The data follows the map (`start_box` captured_airfields / captured_ports_vp).
- **Red flag (pennant) on a counter.** Printed on every Air Support and Naval Support, both Staff Assets, the German Special Action events, Patton Soldier Slap, Axis Tactical Extract, both Special Action blocks, and the Italian XII and XVI engineers (not on any Allied engineer, nor on the US and British Special Action events). The aids do not say what it means; the data records `red_flag: true`.
- **Flanking Invasion (17.22).** The "Flanking Inv" bracket is on the British 3rd CDO (asset matrix: commandos only, once per game, as a Flanking Invasion event) and on both US 30 RCT (-) counters (turns 4 and 5). The event summary calls Flanking Invasion an Allied event. Data: `infantry_asset` with `flanking_invasion` (`once_per_game` on 3rd CDO, `true` on 30 RCT). Are the 30 RCT counters events, assets, or both?
- **Rangers and commandos** (asset matrix: used as infantry assets; option to remove field works before firing, 16.22): 1st/3rd/4th Ranger `subtype: ranger`, 3rd CDO `commando`. **RYL Marines** (X with an anchor, elite, no Flanking Inv bracket) is `subtype: marines`: does it count as a commando?
- **Palermitani** (a square with one diagonal, NATO cavalry/recon) is `infantry_asset`, `symbol: recon`, infantry class. Mobile Groups A-F print the armor oval (`armor_asset`), Mobile G the infantry X.
- **`division` on assets** is a name match only (US 3rd, 45th, 1st, 82nd Airborne, 2nd, 9th; British 5th, 50th, 51st, 1st Canadian, 78th, 1st Airbourne; German 15th Pz Gren, HG Pz, 29th Pz Gren). The matrix says "higher echelon restrictions apply"; the rule itself is in the Play Book.
- **Asset Delayed (17.32): "when drawn, remove from game"**, a new kind, not Bulge's Delayed Response. US 2, 3, 4; Axis 7, 8, 9 (grey, so `nation: german`). Sunrise Bridge puts the green US one (turn 2) in the British turn 2 row.
- **Patton Soldier Slap (17.11)** and **Axis Tactical Extract (17.12)** are printed in the German grey section (`nation: german`). Patton targets the US single-use events (`target: us_7th_army`); Tactical Extract affects German units.
- **US 7th Army Release (17.23)**: "All US divisions may now enter and operate in zones A, C and D", so `event_army_release` with `zones`, not Bulge's single-division `event_release`. How it relates to the six US Restricted markers (map: Restricted Formation Status, 7th Army Restricted / Released) and the Special Action "Allied Single Division Release (14.61)" is in the Play Book.
- **All replacements are Non-Elite** (`elite_allowed: false`): US Inf 2/4/6/8, US Armor 5, British Inf 2/4/6/8.
- **Turn dates.** The map's track: 1 July 10-11, 2 12-13, 3 14-15, 4 16-17, 5 18-19, 6 20-22, 7 23-25, 8 26-28, 9 29-31. The Allied reinforcement card prints turn 7 as "July 26-28" (same as turn 8) and the Axis card turn 3 as "July 14-16"; the data uses the map.
- **Turn track values (for the turn track file, not written here).** Draws first/second Admin Phase: Allied 0/0 on turn 1, then 11/5* every turn (* +1 per Allied-controlled undamaged Sicilian port); Axis 0/0, 10/5, 10/5, 9/5, 8/4, 7/4, 6/3, 5/3, 4/2. Sudden-death net VPs (Allied/Axis) from turn 4: 2/8, 3/7, 4/6, 5/5, 6/4, 7/3. Sunrise Bridge: Allied 0/0 then 6/3; Axis 0/0, 5/3, 5/2, 4/2 (in `setups.json`).
- **Campaign VPs start on 8** (the Vassal setup puts the VPs marker there, Axis face up); port damage markers start at 0. Not confirmed by any aid. The scenario name "campaign" is ours.
- **Marker counts come from the 228-counter sheet,** so two faces share one stock: 9 River Assault / Road Block, 4 Exploitation / Out of Supply, 18 Disordered / Recovering, 23 Field Works / Blown Bridge, 9 Control. The Axis places Field Works "on units starting in Invasion-capable Areas"; which areas those are is for the map / Play Book.
- **Spelling as printed:** "1st Airbourne" on the British counters and Restricted marker (the blocks have no name but "1st"), "Carmito" (Vassal file "Carmite"), "23rd Dismnt.".
- **German FJ battalions** (1-3/3rd FJ turn 2, 1-3/4th FJ turn 4) are `airborne: true`, `subtype: paratroop`; the 1st FJ artillery (turn 5) has the same teal box. The aids mention Airborne Assaults (resolved before other combats) and an Airborne Assault box; which pieces use it is in the Play Book.
- **Sunrise Bridge** (p. 11): the 1st Airborne block of turn 3 has no entry on the card; victory conditions and the red numbered squares on the scenario map (p. 9) are for the map / Play Book. Livorno arrives on turn 2 there although it starts in area 40 in the campaign.
