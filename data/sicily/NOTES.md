# FAB: Sicily data notes

## Units and counters

Built by `tools/sicily/pieces.py` (see `tools/sicily/README.md`). Sources: the Vassal module `docs/games/sicily/other/FAB_Sicily.vmod` (one image per piece; where each piece sits on its three set-up cards; no step or quality data, so pips and codes were read off the images), the owner's scans (`docs/games/sicily/counters`: sticker sheet, counter sheet front and back) and the player aids (`SICILY_PLAYER_AIDS.pdf`: p. 7 Allied reinforcements, p. 10 Allied at start, p. 12 Axis at start and reinforcements, p. 8/9/11 Sunrise Bridge, p. 2 event summary, p. 5 asset matrix, p. 6 fading Italian morale). Rules: Sicily Rule Book 13.0-19.0 and Play Book 22.0 (`tools/rules/sicily-rules.md`, `sicily-playbook.md`). Every Vassal image is reference only: `image` is `null` and `vassal_image(s)` names the module file.

Counts: 73 blocks (US 12, British 15, Allied Special Action 1; Italian 8 + 29 untried Coastal/Garrison, German 7, Axis Special Action 1) and the full 228-counter sheet in 152 entries (US 46 assets and events, British 33, Allied Fading Italian Morale 3, German 24, Italian 24, markers 98). The script checks each entry against the module (image present, number of pieces per image, turn on the set-up card and in the file name, no module image left over) and the 228 total.

### Model

- **Sides `allied` and `axis`; `nation` us / british / german / italian.** The 1st Canadian Division and 1st Canadian Armoured Brigade are `british` (8th Army, "BR" in Vassal) with `canadian: true` (red unit box). `echelon` is the army: `us_7th_army`, `br_8th_army`, and `it_6th_army` for every Axis piece, German included, because the card is "AT START: Italian 6th Army" and the 6th Army Reserve markers serve both. Nation gives the colour (US olive, British tan, Italian orange, German grey). Allied-wide pieces (Special Action block, Fading Italian Morale) have `nation: null`.
- **Blocks:** `quality_by_step[i]` is the quality at i+1 steps, read edge by edge (white pip green, black veteran, red elite). `division` + `size` (division / brigade / garrison), `colours` (stripe, unit box, sticker paper sampled from the Vassal face), `sticker_code` as printed: `Inv` = lands on turn 1 (`arrival: invasion`), a turn number = reinforcement (`arrival_turn`, `entry: null` = not known), an Axis number in the yellow box = `start_area`, `25R` = area 25 with a 6th Army reserve marker (`start_reserve`), `S*` = untried set-up. No block shows a yellow arrow, so none arrives reduced.
- **Kinds reused from The Bulge / Golan:** artillery, engineer, infantry_asset, armor_asset, air_mission (Air Support, as in Golan), special_action_event, special_action_block, command_staff, replacement_inf, replacement_arm, reserve_marker, marker, marker_control, field_works. **New:** `untried` (block kind), `naval_support`, `event_asset_delayed`, `event_fading_morale`, `event_army_release`, `event_patton_slap`, `event_tactical_extract`, `marker_restricted`, `marker_beachhead`, `marker_port_damage`. `subtype` marks ranger, commando, marines, paratroop, bersaglieri, blackshirt, mobile_group; `flanking_invasion`, `airborne`, `red_flag`, `division` are flags read off the counter.
- **Turn codes:** `S` start (Available Box), number = turn, `C` conditional (`conditional` + `start_box`), markers `null`. `reinforcements.json` lists both sides per turn plus `invasion`, `untried` and `conditional`; `setups.json` has `sunrise_bridge`, `tournament` and `campaign`.
- **Backs:** Allied assets green "Asset or Event" (82), Axis grey (48, Italian included). Two-faced markers carry the other face as `back`: Control (Allied star / Axis cross), Game Turn, VPs, port damage 1/2, River Assault / Road Block, Exploitation / Out of Supply, Disordered / Recovering, Field Works / Blown Bridge, Restricted / Released. Reserve and Beach Head markers print the same face on both sides (`back: "same"`).

### Settled by the Rule Book and Play Book

- 13.1 First player: the Allies, in every scenario. 22.27: the Tournament and Campaign start with the Allied Operational Movement Phase; only the Allied Admin Phase of turn 1 is skipped (both sides draw 0/0 on turn 1 anyway). Sunrise Bridge keeps the card's "skip first Admin Phase" for both sides.
- 13.2 Block wood: Italian 38 light green (Axis Special Action included), German 7 dark grey, US 12 dark green, British 16 dark blue (Allied Special Action included). The 3D table uses the owner's colours: German grey, US olive, British blue as in The Bulge, Italian orange #e66525.
- 13.3 Classes: Mechanized Infantry is armor class (Panzergrenadier, HG); Mountain (Assietta), Airborne, Motorized (Livorno), Garrison and Ranger are infantry class. 15.1: Livorno has 6 MP unless it starts in or enters a contested area, and always fights as infantry.
- 13.4 E Red flag = Strategic Use: a strategic asset may support either Allied army (16.2); army-specific assets only help their own army, and 14.31 also lets an Allied asset support units of its own nationality outside the army's zones. `C` = conditional (Air Support, Fading Italian Morale). The asset `division` names are for show only; the restriction is by army.
- 13.4 F, 16.22-16.23, 17.22 Rangers and commandos: Rangers and 3rd CDO may remove the defender's field works instead of rolling. 3rd CDO and both 30 RCT (-) are battle assets that can also be played as a Flanking Invasion event in an Admin Phase (3rd CDO once per game, 30 RCT each time it is available). RYL Marines is not listed: a plain battle asset.
- 14.21 Invasion-capable areas are the 23 areas a landing box points at (A1-A4, B1-B6, D1-D13), not every coastal area. Field works go on those holding an Axis unit (22.14, 22.24).
- 14.24-14.25 Entry: Axis only through port box P4 Messina; US on a US beachhead or P3 Palermo; British on a British beachhead or P1 Syracuse / P2 Catania (ports undamaged and friendly). Airborne units (82nd and 1st Airbourne, two blocks each) go to the Airborne Assault box and drop at the start of combat. `entry` in units.json and reinforcements.json now says this; the German FJ counters are assets and do not drop.
- 14.31, 14.41, 22.25-22.26 Zones: the scenario's landing plan gives each army its zones. Historical: British 5 consecutive boxes in zone B, zone B (released: A, B, C); US boxes A1-A4, zones A and C (released: A, C, D). Alternate: British 5 consecutive boxes across A/B, zones A and B (released: + C); US 4 consecutive D boxes, zones C and D (released: + A). US never in zone B except Messina; British never in zone D. Release by Special Action (14.61, one division, plus one independent British brigade) or US 7th Army Release (17.23, all US divisions).
- 14.9 Supply: Axis from Messina (friendly-controlled); Allies from a beachhead of their own nationality or any friendly undamaged port. Sunrise Bridge: Axis from areas 16, 17, 25 or 26.
- 15.3 Untried garrisons: placed per scenario. Sunrise Bridge one each in 1-7, 13, 14, 26; Tournament and Campaign anywhere, one per area, not where two tried units already stand; Campaign historical option: the 23 invasion-capable areas plus 26, 28, 29, 30, 48, 49.
- 16.4 and 17.21 The map is right: the conditional Air Support start in the Captured Airfields boxes, the Fading Italian Morale events in Captured Ports and Key VP Areas.
- 16.5 Each undamaged port the Allies control and occupy adds one to both Allied Admin draws.
- 17.11 Patton Soldier Slap: from then on a US single-use Special Action costs two events. 17.12 Tactical Extract: German units may move contested-to-adjacent for one Movement Phase, not away from Messina, and end Recovering. 17.32 Asset Delayed: eliminated when drawn, still counts as a draw. 17.24-17.25: replacements add one non-elite step to a reduced unit.
- 19.1-19.4 Victory: German crosses (28, 29 x3, 30) Allied only and only if occupied; white stars (5, 57, 65) Axis only; yellow stars (15, 18, 26, 45, 49) both, the Allies only if occupied; an objective must also trace supply. Eliminated 3+ step units and captured beachheads are 1 VP each. At start the Axis holds 3 white + 5 yellow = 8 VPs, which matches the Vassal marker on 8. Sudden death from turn 4 per the turn track. Tournament: Axis 4+ wins, else Allied. Campaign: 4+ operational, 1-3 marginal, 0 draw. Sunrise Bridge: the red squares on the scenario map are its VPs (22.17), no large-unit VPs, positive net VPs wins.
- Scenarios in setups.json: `sunrise_bridge` (22.1, 4 turns), `tournament` (22.2, 5 turns, July 10-19), `campaign` (22.3, 9 turns, with the historical option).

- **Italian block colour.** 13.2 says light green wood; the 3D table uses your orange #e66525. Keep orange?

### Kept for reference

- **Turn track values.** Draws first/second Admin Phase: Allied 0/0 on turn 1, then 11/5* every turn (* +1 per Allied-controlled, occupied, undamaged Sicilian port, 16.5); Axis 0/0, 10/5, 10/5, 9/5, 8/4, 7/4, 6/3, 5/3, 4/2. Sudden-death net VPs (Allied/Axis) from turn 4: 2/8, 3/7, 4/6, 5/5, 6/4, 7/3. Sunrise Bridge: Allied 0/0 then 6/3; Axis 0/0, 5/3, 5/2, 4/2 (in `setups.json`).
- **Marker counts come from the 228-counter sheet,** so two faces share one stock: 9 River Assault / Road Block, 4 Exploitation / Out of Supply, 18 Disordered / Recovering, 23 Field Works / Blown Bridge, 9 Control.
- **25R and the two 6th Army Reserve markers.** 13.4 A: "Starting Area No. in Reserve". The data puts M03a on GE01 (15th PG, area 25) and M03b in the Axis Reserve Holding Box; Vassal starts both in the box.
- **Untried Coastal/Garrison blocks: 24 one-step green, 4 two-step green, 1 two-step veteran** (15.3 b: "the one veteran untried unit"). Vassal's 29 "?" helper markers are not physical pieces and are not in the data.

## Map

The game map is now `data/sicily/map.json` + `map_art.json`, read from the owner's hand-drawn layered SVG (`docs/games/sicily/maps/SicilyMap.svg`) by `tools/sicily/svgmap.py`; the Vassal draft below is kept for the area ids and names and as a cross-check (every one of its 65 areas, 144 connections, 25 rivers, 7 restricted edges, 25 army-boundary edges, zones, VPs, ports, airfields and 27 boxes agrees with the drawing). The engine gives the landing boxes the ids 101-123 (A1-A4, B1-B6, D1-D13) and the port boxes 131-134 (P1-P4).

Draft model `data/sicily/map_vassal.json`, built by `tools/sicily/zones.py`, `segment.py` and `mapdraft.py` (see `tools/sicily/README.md`), as `data/golan73/map_vassal.json` was for Golan: a cross-check for the hand-drawn SVG to come. Sources: the module's "Main Map" (image 3332 x 2156 px, 145 zones), the printed map scan `docs/games/sicily/maps/SICILYmap-FINAL-72.jpg`, the Sunrise Bridge scenario map (player aids p. 9), the legend and the movement table (aids p. 3). The printed scan is the same map as the module's, scaled by 1/1.361 (fitted at 0.5 px rms); compared pixel by pixel, the two differ only in ink colour, so every value below holds on both. The Sunrise Bridge map agrees with both on its 17 areas (boundaries, roads, rivers, plates, symbols).

Counts: 65 areas; 144 shared edges (72 road, 72 field), 25 of them river (11 with a road), 7 restricted, 25 army boundary; 14 corner-only contacts; 34 coastal areas; 23 landing boxes, 4 port boxes (3 damage boxes), 52 other boxes and track spaces (50 Vassal zones + 2 printed boxes with no zone).

### Model

- **Areas:** `id`, `name` as printed (Vassal name kept in `vassal_name`), `tdn` from the number plate (legend: circle 0, square 1, hexagon 2, triangle 3; 21 / 24 / 19 / 1), `vp` {allied, german, mutual}, `zone` (army zone A-D, see below), `coastal` (touches the sea), `port` (P1-P4), `airfield`, `beaches` (landing boxes whose arrow points at it), `axis_exit` (Messina), `sunrise_bridge`, `label` (pole of inaccessibility), `plate` (centre of the printed number plate), `polygon` (traced on the map image, px) and `vassal_polygon` (the module zone, coarser and spilling into the sea).
- **The map shows no terrain type** beyond the TDN plate and the relief shading: no town, city, mountain or forest symbols, so there is no terrain field. Mount Etna (27) is the only TDN 3.
- **Connections:** `type` road (1 MP) or field (2 MP), `river`, `restricted`, `army_boundary` with the two `zones`, `length` (px of shared edge), `mid`, `road_at` (where the road crosses). Shaped like The Bulge's `map.json` edges.
- **Symbols found:** Allied VP crosses in 28 Taormina (1), 30 Novara (1), 29 Messina (3 crosses); German VP stars in 5 Syracuse, 57 Palermo, 65 Marsala; mutual VP pentagons in 15 Val di Catania, 18 Niscemi, 26 Catania, 45 Caltanissetta, 49 Termini Imerese; airfields in 18 Niscemi, 26 Catania, 65 Marsala (the Captured Airfields chart has 3 spaces); ports with an anchor and a box: P1 Syracuse (5, "Allied Control Required (8th Army only)", damage box), P2 Catania (26, same text, damage box), P3 Palermo (57, "Allied Control Required (7th Army only)", damage box), P4 Messina (29, "Axis Control Required (6th Army only)", no damage box). Three white arrows from Messina to the grey mainland, labelled "Exiting Axis Units".
- **No supply source and no entry area is printed** on the map: both come from the rules (14.9, 14.25).
- **Other boxes:** turn track 1-9 (dates, Admin Phase draw "1st/2nd" for each side, sudden-death net VPs "Allied/Axis", copied into `turn_track`), General Information Track 0-9, Eliminated Large Units 1-10, Fading Italian Morale track (At Start, Level 1-3), Captured Airfields and Captured Ports and Key VP Areas charts, Restricted Formation Status (7th / 8th Army, Restricted / Released), Allied and Axis Available / Used / Eliminated boxes and Reserve Marker Holding Boxes, At Sea, Airborne Assault, the Allied cup ("Allied Resource Draw Cup" in Vassal, a green mug). The grey Axis mug (selection cup) and the mainland ("Exiting Axis Units") have no Vassal zone; their rectangles were read by eye.

### Settled by the rules

- 14.1 Restricted terrain: exactly the 7 edges found (31/32, 33/34 and every edge of Mount Etna, 27). Entering costs all MP and armor class may not cross; the side that controls an uncontested area 27 may use its roads (1 MP, armor too).
- 14.41 Messina (29) is in both zone B and zone C (`zone: null` in the draft; the engine will treat it as B and C).
- 14.21 Invasion-capable = the 23 areas a landing box points at (`beaches`), not the 34 coastal areas. D1-D13 are the Alternate Landing Plan's US beaches (22.26) and Flanking Invasion targets are any enemy coastal area (17.22). Ladbroke (B5) and Fustian (B6) are ordinary landing boxes: the Campaign's historical option uses B1-B5.
- 14.23 There are four ports (P1-P4); Marsala is not one. Messina cannot be damaged (14.51).
- 19.1 VP symbols: 1 VP per symbol (Messina's three crosses = 3), see the units notes above.
- No supply source or entry area is printed on the map: both come from 14.9 and 14.25 (Messina; beachheads and ports).

### Still open

- **The army boundary follows rivers where no dashes are drawn (8 edges).** The dashed line ends on a river at 15/16 and continues along the Simeto as 16/26 (A/B); between zones A and D and C and D it runs along the Salso and its branches: 20/41, 21/40, 39/40, 39/45, 38/45, 38/46 (A/D) and 37/46 (C/D). Evidence: the "ZONE D / ZONE A" labels are printed along the Salso at 20/41, the dashes stop where the rivers start, and flood-filling the map over the non-boundary edges gives exactly four zones: A = 7-11, 16-25, 38, 39; B = 1-6, 12-15, 26-28; C = 30-37; D = 40-65. The Sunrise Bridge zone A list (7, 10, 11, 16, 17, 25) agrees that 16 is zone A. Please draw these on your new map.
- **Rivers and bridges.** No bridge symbol is printed; under the 2016 series rules every river boundary has a bridge until blown, so there is no bridge field. 11 river edges also carry a road; 14/26 on the Simeto (Catania / Lentini) is the Primosole bridge, the "Sunrise Bridge" of the scenario.
- **Corner-only contacts are not adjacent:** 7/11, 10/17, 10/19, 11/16, 16/24, 21/41, 22/24, 24/33, 24/37, 25/27, 32/34 (and 31/33, same four-way point), 36/46, 40/44, 54/56. The Vassal zones overlap at 25/27 and would make Regalbuto and Mount Etna neighbours; on the map they meet at one point. The shortest real edge is 58/63 (Alcamo / San Vito lo Capo, about 38 px), kept as adjacent.
- **Spelling.** Names are kept as printed: "Palazzalo Acreide" (Palazzolo Acreide), "Catalafimi" (Calatafimi), "Capo di Orlando" (Capo d'Orlando, also so in 14.1), "Canicatti" (Canicattì). Correct them on the new map?
