# SEQUENCE OF PLAY

Card: A

## Start of Turn Phase

### Reinforcement Phase – Both Players

- Both players – follow steps in holding boxes on map

## First Player Turn

### Admin Phase – Both Players

- Draw assets/events from Selection Cup
  - See turn record track on map
- Phasing Player _first_ then Non-Phasing Player
  - Play and resolve events
  - Perform engineering functions
  - Perform staff functions
  - Use Special Action(s)

### Operational Movement Phase – Phasing Player

- Place units in reserve, move, build field works, or hold position.

### Strategic Movement Phase – Phasing Player

- Move units marked with a reserve marker (as allowed)

### Combat Phase – For each area as required or desired

- **Defender Special Action** – Reinforce or retreat
- **Commit Assets** – Attacker then Defender
- **Declare Point Unit** – Defender then Attacker
- **Reveal Units** – Both
- **Artillery Fire** – Attacker then Defender
- **Ground Fire** – Defender then Attacker

### Reaction & Breakthrough Phase – Both Players

- **Reaction** – Non-phasing Player
- **Breakthrough Movement** – Phasing Player
  - Units in reserve
  - Exploitation units
  - Armor Class units via Special Action
- **Breakthrough Combat** – Phasing Player
  - Limit of one combat round per area

### Supply Phase – Both Players

- Verify control of areas
- Check supply status of all units
- Adjust disorder & recovery markers
- Morale check for Phasing Player's unsupplied units
- End of turn marker removal

## Second Player Turn

- Second Player becomes Phasing Player/Attacker
- Repeat First Player Turn sequence above

## Victory Check Phase

- Mark current VP level
- Check for Sudden Death Victory

### Movement Restrictions

- **Sequence** – Move one Unit at a time
- **Entry** – Must **_stop_** when entering an area with enemy:
  - Unit or commando asset, or garrison asset
  - Roadblock or defended roadblock
- **Exit** – When exiting enemy occupied area, must exit **_directly into_** friendly uncontested area
- **Exit** – Cannot exit enemy occupied area _and_ re-enter another enemy occupied area in the same turn
- **Restricted Crossing in all cases of movement**
  - Only 1 unit may cross an Escarpment or Rugged boundary
- **Restricted Crossing into Contested Area**
  - Only 1 unit may cross into a contested area if the boundary is: Anti-Tank Ditch or Wadi
- **Crossing a Bridge into Contested Area**
  - **_Only 1 unit_** may cross the bridge (whether intact or blown)
  - If area is **_newly_** contested, Defender may try to blow the bridge during the **_first_** bridge-crossing attempt into the area
- **Strategic Movement** – Move any number of areas, however…
  - May only enter friendly-controlled areas connected by roads
  - Cannot enter areas with enemy units/assets/roadblocks
  - Cannot cross an unbridged Anti-Tank Ditch boundary
  - Cannot cross blown bridges
- **Strategic Movement** – Limits per Game Turn
  - Syrian: limited only by number of Reserve markers
  - Israeli: limited only by number of Reserve markers
- **Night Turn – Both Sides**
  - No unit can cross a prohibited boundary or move to/from Mt Hermon
  - Strat-move limits: 2 (Israeli) or 1 (Syrian)
  - No air assault & no air assets may be used
- **Night Turn – Israeli Units & Syrian Armor Class Units**
  - Israeli: units move as normal
  - Syrian: units can move up to 2 areas expending no more than _4 MP_
- **Night Turn – Syrian Infantry Class units**
  - Units can move only one area

# EXTENDED SEQUENCE OF PLAY

Card: B

## Start of Turn Phase

### Reinforcement Phase – Both Players

- If the reusable Special Action was not used, move one asset directly from Used Box to Available Box (4.1a)
- Otherwise move the reusable Special Action from Used Box to Available Box (4.1a)
- Syrian Player: Reduction of SAM Barrier (16.12) – may affect one SAM asset
- Move all remaining assets from Used Box into selection cup (4.1b)

### Arriving Resources

- **Units** placed in entry areas (4.1c & 8.27 & 14.21 & 14.22)
- **Assets and Events** placed into selection cup (4.1d)

## First Player Turn

### Admin Phase – Both Players

- **_Both players_** randomly draw a number of assets/events from selection cup as indicated on the Turn Track (4.2a)
- **_Phasing Player first then Non-phasing Player:_**
  - Perform engineering functions (4.2 & 4.3)
  - Play and resolve events (4.2 & 17.0)
  - Play and resolve commando raid assets (16.11)
  - Play and resolve SAM air defense fire assets (16.12)
  - Play and resolve air support assets (16.31)
  - Perform staff functions (4.2 & 8.3)
  - Use Special Actions (8.25 & 8.26)

### Operational Movement Phase – Phasing Player

**Movement Action**

- Place units in reserve, move, build field works, or hold position (5.21, 5.22, 5.23 & 5.24); Night – movement restricted (14.03)

**River Assaults & Crossing Assaults**

- Mark as appropriate (5.35e & 14.01a)

**Overstacking (involuntary only)**

- Correct at end of Phase by retreating units (5.1 & 6.6)

### Strategic Movement Phase – Phasing Player

**Reserve Move**

- Move units marked with a reserve marker per Strategic Movement Rules; Night – strat-move restricted (14.03)
- Remove reserve marker from units so moved (5.4)

### Combat Phase – For each area as required or desired, conducting Air Assault (15.3) before all other combats; Night – no Air Assaults and no use of air assets (14.03)

**Special Action Decision**

- Defender may reinforce or retreat with Special Action (6.21)

**Commit Assets**

- Attacker assigns assets to this battle (6.22)
- Defender assigns assets to this battle (6.23)

**Declare Point**

- Both declare point unit (Defender first) (6.24)

**Reveal Units**

- Both reveal units if a newly contested area (6.24)

**Artillery Fire**

- Attacking artillery fires (6.25) – _Defender applies hits_ (6.42)
- Defending artillery fires (6.26) – _Attacker applies hits_ (6.41)

**Ground Fire**

- Ground units and battle assets fire in the following order:
  - Defender fires (6.27) – _Attacker applies hits_ (6.41)
  - Attacker fires (unless attack aborted) (6.28) – _Defender applies hits_ (6.42)
- Both place remaining assets from this combat in Used Box (6.29)

### Reaction & Breakthrough Phase – Both Players

**Reaction**

- Non-phasing Player moves reserves (7.2)

**Breakthrough Movement**

- Phasing Player moves these units in any desired order:
  - Units in reserve (7.3)
  - Exploitation units, but only into adjacent areas (7.3 & 7.31)
  - Armor Class units, by spending a Special Action for each unit, but only into adjacent areas (7.3 & 7.31 & 8.21)

**Breakthrough Combat**

- Combats caused by Breakthrough Movement are resolved as in the regular Combat Phase, with a limit of one combat round per area (7.4)

### Supply Phase – Both Players

**Control of Areas**

- Both players verify control of areas (9.2)

**Supply**

- Players check supply status of all units (9.31, 14.93 & 14.94), and place/remove unsupplied markers (9.31 & 9.32)

**Disorder & Recovery**

- Players remove recovering markers from all units and then flip disordered markers to the recovering side (9.35)

**Morale Check – _Phasing Player Only_**

- Phasing Player makes morale checks for his unsupplied units (9.4)

**Marker Removal**

- Players remove _all_ markers as follows (9.5):
  - All reserve markers from Non-phasing Player units
  - All field works markers from contested areas
  - All road blocks and defended road blocks
  - All river assault markers

## Second Player Turn

- Second Player becomes Phasing Player/Attacker
- First Player becomes Non-phasing Player/Defender
- Repeat the Phases outlined for the First Player Turn above

## Victory Check Phase

- Determine and mark Current Victory Point Level (19.3)
- Check for Sudden Death Victory (19.4)

## Game Turn Advancement

- If no Sudden Death Victory, advance turn marker to the next game turn

## Combat Results

**a)** Follow the Hit Priorities sequence above (per 6.41 & 6.42)

**b)** Apply step losses and disorder results

**c)** Determine final results per below; in ALL cases:

- surviving assets are moved to the Used Box
- any units in areas no longer contested return to hidden status

**Area Remains Contested** – Neither side eliminated; units remain face up

**Defender Retreated** – Surviving Attacker Unit(s) gain control of the area

**Attacker Eliminated** – Surviving Defender Unit(s) maintain control of the area

**Defender Eliminated by**

- **Artillery** – Exploitation opportunity
- **Ground Fire with at least 1 Excess Hit** – Exploitation opportunity

**Exploitation** – Armor Class units must pass a morale check (SN +2 at Night); exploitation cannot happen during a River (Crossing or Air) Assault

## Disordered/Unsupplied

**Standard Effects** (9.33)

- Cannot initiate combat
- SN modifier when firing and for morale checks
- Can only move 1 area, cannot build field works, cannot be placed in reserve

**Additional Effects – Unsupplied** (9.34 & 9.4)

- Assets, Special Actions, engineering functions and friendly events cannot be played for such units / areas
- May not recover from disordered status
- Must make morale check during friendly Supply Phase: Failure results in disordered result, a step loss and possible surrender
