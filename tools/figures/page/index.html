Figure Workshop

Each figure type goes through six steps and ends as a low-poly figure: a few hundred flat faces in plain colours, like a painted war-game miniature. AI services draw the picture and build the 3D model; you choose and approve; Claude turns the model low-poly and puts it in the game. Open a type to see its work order.
